# ADR 007: Research and tactical mandates

Date: 2026-10-22

Status: **Accepted — data and authority boundary**

## Context

A short-term setup can disagree with long-term thesis; combining them hides horizon and risk.

## Decision

Keep separate mandate, setup, thesis and decision records. Committee preserves dissent and independent risk references. Portfolio aggregates exposure across mandates but never duplicates underlying transactions.

## Alternatives

A single buy/sell score erases evidence, timing and risk differences. Separate databases would fragment identity and holdings truth.

## Consequences and limits

P0 has contracts only. No trading, sizing, order submission or real account state is implemented.

## Approval and migration

Risk limits and actual account policies require owner approval before actionable sizing. Broker execution requires an entirely separate future scope.
